ReelPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary: ReelPilot does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your own browser.

What ReelPilot is

ReelPilot is a Chrome extension that controls playback of Reels on instagram.com. It moves to the next reel automatically and adds playback controls (play/pause, next/previous, speed, volume, Picture-in-Picture), including while the tab is in the background.

Information ReelPilot handles

  • Your settings (auto-scroll mode, plays per reel, timer, speed, volume, panel position and similar preferences). These are saved with Chrome's extension storage on your device.
  • Page content, in memory only. To work, ReelPilot reads parts of the Instagram page you're viewing: the reel video, the reel's author name and whether the reel is labelled as an ad. It uses this only to show it in its controls and to decide when to move to the next reel. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Session state for the toolbar badge (whether auto-scroll is on, how many reels you've watched this session and the current reel's author name). This is kept in Chrome's session storage on your device and is cleared when you close the browser.

What ReelPilot does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • It does not read your messages, your account details, your passwords or your browsing on other websites.
  • It does not like, follow, comment or post anything on your behalf.
  • It does not sell or transfer user data, and does not use it for any purpose unrelated to its single purpose, including creditworthiness or lending.

Permissions

  • Access to instagram.com: to show the controls on Instagram and operate the reel videos there. ReelPilot runs on no other website.
  • Storage: to remember your settings.
  • Scripting: to start ReelPilot in Instagram tabs that were already open when you install or update it, so you don't have to reload them.

Removing your data

Uninstalling ReelPilot from chrome://extensions removes its settings from your browser.
